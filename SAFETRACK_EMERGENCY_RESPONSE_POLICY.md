# SafeTrack emergency response policy

SafeTrack does not operate a control room and does not contact the South African Police Service, an ambulance service, or a rescue service.

When a person needs emergency help they call 10111 or 112 themselves. The SOS button records an incident and queues a notice to contacts who already have permission. The interface says the notice is queued and that it was not delivered by a phone network, because no SMS or push provider is connected.

A missed journey check-in asks the traveller first by recording `asked_subject`. It does not open a missing-person incident.

A missing-person form is available without waiting for staff. The page shows, before and after submission: report the person to SAPS immediately and do not wait 24 hours. A police station and case reference can be stored. SafeTrack does not check that reference with SAPS.

Staff must not tell a family that help has been dispatched.

Official access stays off for outside organisations until a named agency is inserted as verified after a real agreement. No such row is seeded.
