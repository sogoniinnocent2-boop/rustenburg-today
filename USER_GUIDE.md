# User guide

Open the site and choose English, Setswana or Afrikaans. The choice is kept in a cookie and, after you sign in, on your profile.

## Account

Create an account with your name, email, a password of at least 10 characters including a letter and a number, and your date of birth. Accept the terms. If you are under 18, confirm that a parent or guardian agrees. Under 13, registration stops.

Until email is connected, a development server shows a one-time verification link. On a public server that link will not be shown; you will need the email. After verification, sign in. One account covers news, community, jobs, learning and the other desks.

From Account you can edit your profile, read notifications, open messages, review privacy, change your password, export your data or delete the account. Deletion removes your name from public use and retires the email address.

You can add a seller, employer, business, advertiser, creator, podcaster, instructor, organiser or service-provider role yourself. Journalist, editor and moderator are requests. An administrator decides those.

## Reading and posting

News and podcasts that are published appear on the home page. Empty departments say nothing is published yet. That is intentional.

On a news or department story page you can react the way people do on Facebook: Like, Love, Care, Haha, Wow, Sad or Angry. Sign in first. Choosing the same reaction again removes it.

Community posts can be an update, discussion, poll, safety report or lost-and-found note. Safety reports stay pending for review. Do not post an identity number, private address or an allegation you cannot support.

## Content desks (department staff)

Department accounts belong to the people who keep this website informative. Open **https://www.rustenburgtoday.co.za/department**, sign in, then choose your desk. Publish headlines, pictures and story updates so the home page and section pages stay current. One account can hold several departments. News desk stories sit beside SAnews headline links; they do not replace the official feed.

The desk shows a story report with views and Facebook-style reaction totals for that department. Download CSV exports the same numbers for Excel or Sheets.

## Work, learning and trade

Jobs, courses, events, businesses, adverts and classifieds are created from the department’s Create link. Most stay hidden until the desk approves them, unless you publish from an assigned department desk. An order is a record of what you asked to buy. It stays unpaid until a payment provider confirms it on the server. There is no live checkout yet.

Course completion certificates from this site are records of finishing the lessons here. They are not a formal qualification.

## Ask, briefing and the map

Ask searches what is already published. If it is not listed, it is not confirmed. The daily briefing needs a signed-in account and uses the same published records. The map shows reviewed points with a source and a time. It does not show private homes or unverified claims.

## Help

Terms, privacy and community guidelines are linked in the footer. Use Report on a post or listing that breaks those rules.
