# SafeTrack user guide

Open SafeTrack from the account menu after you sign in. You need an adult account. People under 13 cannot register, and a dependant profile has no password.

Create a household and invite another adult by the email on their account. When they join, they still cannot see your location.

Grant a permission by email. Choose one: SOS, journey alerts, latest location, last known location, profile, or emergency information. The list on the page shows who has access. Withdraw removes it.

Add an emergency contact when you want them named for emergencies. Also grant "Receive SOS" if they should be notified. The contact is not a guardian.

SOS records an incident immediately. The result tells you the reference, how many notices were queued, and that they are not marked delivered. Call 10111 or 112 yourself if you need emergency services.

If someone may be missing, the page tells you to report them to the South African Police Service immediately and not to wait 24 hours. You can store a station and a case reference. Marking the person located updates SafeTrack only.

A journey records where you said you were going and when you expect to arrive. If that time passes, the system asks you to check in. It does not declare you missing. The website cannot follow a phone in the background.

Children: register a dependant, then wait for verification. Location sharing stays off, and when a verified guardian turns it on the household page says so.
