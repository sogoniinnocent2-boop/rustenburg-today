# Product requirements

Product: Rustenburg Today  
Tagline: Your city. Your stories. Your opportunities.  
Positioning: The digital city  
Version: 1.0  
Date: 16 November 2026  
Place: Rustenburg, North West, South Africa  
Languages: English (`en`), Setswana (`tn`), Afrikaans (`af`)  
First platform: responsive web. Android and iOS are later.

## Purpose

One destination for residents, businesses, journalists, learners, employers, creators and visitors. News, community, podcasts, television, radio, talent, advertising, classifieds, jobs, courses, events, a business directory and the other city desks share one account, one search index, one notification centre and one admin desk.

## Principles that constrain the build

1. A screen is finished only when the workflow persists, authorises, validates, fails visibly and can be administered.
2. One person has one identity. Roles are capabilities, not extra accounts.
3. Mobile layout first, with a light editorial theme and a dark theme. Colours are forest `#102B27`, emerald `#176B55`, gold `#E9B85C`, warm white `#F5F4EF`, charcoal `#263746`, night `#101C1A`, muted `#718078`.
4. Money is integer cents. A browser response never marks a payment successful.
5. Ask answers from stored records only.
6. The site is not an official municipal channel.
7. Sponsored material is labelled.
8. Health pages are a directory, not clinical advice.
9. Course certificates are completion records, not accredited qualifications, unless a provider later proves accreditation.
10. Video and music stay with a licensed host or a rights confirmation. The app does not invent a streaming server.
11. Children under 13 cannot register. Minors cannot be put in open direct messages. Sensitive notices need consent. Identity numbers are not published.
12. Machine translation is not published as editorially approved copy.

## Departments

News, podcasts, Rustenburg TV, talent, advertising, marketplace, learning, jobs, events, business directory, property, food, sports, youth, community support, transport, tourism, health, public voice, radio and music, deals, competitions, history, local services, agriculture, faith, family, technology, public notices, and creators.

Cross-cutting: community feed, messages, search, alerts, map, Ask, daily briefing, and a later rewards ledger.

## Completion

A feature is VERIFIED only when the interface, database, server logic, permissions, validation, empty and error states, persistence, a real user test, admin controls, security checks and docs all exist. Status lives in IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md. This requirements file does not claim that every department has reached that bar.
